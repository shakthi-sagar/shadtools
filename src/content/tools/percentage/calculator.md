## How to calculate percentages

Percentages represent fractions of 100. Percentage calculations are essential for financial planning, tip calculations, discount shopping, statistics, and business growth analytics.

### Common percentage formulas
- **Finding Percentage of a Number**: `Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Total`
- **Finding Percentage Proportion**: `Percentage = (Value ÷ Total) × 100`
- **Finding Percentage Change / Growth**: `Growth % = ((Final - Initial) ÷ Initial) × 100`
