## Metric vs Imperial length units

Length conversions bridge the gap between two major measurement systems: the International System of Units (Metric) used globally, and the Imperial/US Customary System used primarily in the United States and international aviation/maritime contexts.

### Key conversion factors
- **1 Meter (m)** = 3.28084 Feet (ft) = 1.09361 Yards (yd) = 100 Centimeters (cm)
- **1 Kilometer (km)** = 0.621371 Miles (mi) = 1,000 Meters (m)
- **1 Inch (in)** = 2.54 Centimeters (cm) = 25.4 Millimeters (mm)
- **1 Foot (ft)** = 12 Inches (in) = 0.3048 Meters (m)
