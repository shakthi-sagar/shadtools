## Temperature conversion formulas

Temperature scales measure kinetic energy in matter. Celsius is used in metric countries, Fahrenheit is standard in the United States, and Kelvin is standard in thermodynamics and scientific physics.

### Standard formulas
- **Celsius to Fahrenheit**: `°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32`
- **Fahrenheit to Celsius**: `°C = (°F - 32) × 5/9`
- **Celsius to Kelvin**: `K = °C + 273.15`
- **Kelvin to Celsius**: `°C = K - 273.15`
