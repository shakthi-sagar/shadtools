## Metric vs Imperial weight conversion

Weight conversion translates mass measurements between metric units (kilograms, grams, milligrams) and imperial units (pounds, ounces, stone).

### Common weight equivalencies
- **1 Kilogram (kg)** = 2.20462 Pounds (lbs) = 35.274 Ounces (oz) = 1,000 Grams (g)
- **1 Pound (lb)** = 0.453592 Kilograms (kg) = 16 Ounces (oz)
- **1 Stone (st)** = 14 Pounds (lbs) = 6.35029 Kilograms (kg)
